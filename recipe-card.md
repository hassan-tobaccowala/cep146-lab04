# Simple Chocolate Cake
## Ingredients
- Sugar
- Flour
- Cocoa
- Baking powder
- Baking soda
- Salt
- Eggs
- Milk
- Oil
- Vanilla extract
- Boiling water
## Cooking Steps
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees, grease and flour baking pans
2. Stir sugar, flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl
3. Add eggs, milk, oil and vanilla; mix for 2 minutes on medium speed with an electric mixer
4. Stir in the boiling water
5. Pour evenly into prepared pans
6. Bake about 30 to 35 minutes. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely

**Important Cooking Tips!**

- **When checking to see if it is done, insert a toothpick into the center and see if it comes out clean. It's not ready if any cake sticks to it!**

- **The batter will be thing when you stir in the boiling water, don't worry!**

- **It's best to use two nine-inch round baking pans.**
